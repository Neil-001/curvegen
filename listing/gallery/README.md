# Gallery

Screenshots for the galleries on Modrinth and CurseForge, with the title and description for each. The `Listing` workflow uploads them to Modrinth in this order, reading the titles and descriptions from this file. On CurseForge, upload them by hand. Titles fit Modrinth's limit of 64 characters and descriptions its 255.

All are 1920 by 1080. The first seven were taken in Minecraft 26.3 with the default textures. The painted shapes from 8 on are drawn by `listing/art/render.py` from the mod's own solver and painter, each block in the colour of its texture, as the G menu's "Block colours" picture draws them.

## 1. Ringed planet

`01-planet.jpg`

An ellipsoid for the planet, a torus squashed flat for its ring and a small ellipsoid for the moon. The mod picks slabs and stairs where a full block would stick out.

## 2. Editing a Bézier curve

`02-tentacles.jpg`

Tentacles from 3D Bézier curves. Three are placed. The fourth is still a hologram, with a handle on each of its six points and on its box. Drag a handle and the blocks follow.

## 3. Gyroid from an equation

`03-gyroid.jpg`

A 3D equation in x, y and z: a gyroid cut to a ball, 65 blocks across. Anything you can write as an equation or inequality can be built.

## 4. Armillary sphere

`04-armillary.jpg`

Three 2D ellipses, one flat and two upright, each three blocks deep, around a small ellipsoid. Slabs, stairs, trapdoors and shelves round off the rings.

## 5. Radial menu

`05-wave.jpg`

A Bézier surface shaped into a breaking wave. Hold V for the radial menu, which changes the shape's options and blocks, places it or exports it to Litematica without leaving the world.

## 6. Full menu

`06-full-menu.jpg`

Press G for every setting. Here it shows a Bézier surface's size, thickness and control points beside a 3D preview you can turn and zoom. Drag a point in the preview to move it.

## 7. Choosing blocks by colour

`07-block-picker.jpg`

The block list for one piece type, sorted by how close each block's texture is to the block chosen now. Search by name, or sort by name instead.

## 8. Painted dome

`08-dome.png`

The top half of a hollow ellipsoid, painted with the Stripes pattern three blocks wide. The stripes wrap from the top down, and the slabs and stairs are quartz and blackstone like the blocks beside them.

## 9. Two-lane road

`09-road.png`

The Two-lane road pattern on a flat Bézier line 9 blocks wide. It repeats along the curve and stretches across it once, so the edge lines and the dashed centre line follow every bend.

## 10. Gradient torus

`10-torus.png`

A gradient from yellow to blue, painted bottom to top round a torus with ragged edges. Gradients are made and saved in Chroma Studio 1.2 or newer.

## 11. Lattice dome with holes

`11-lattice.png`

The Lattice pattern on a dome, with its empty spots left open. The blocks beside each hole settle as the game would settle them, and holes place nothing and clear nothing.

## 12. Spiral round a tube

`12-spiral.png`

A hollow 3D Bézier curve with open ends, painted with stripes turned 35°. Round a closed loop the pattern meets itself, so the turned stripes become one clean spiral.

## 13. Copper mix

`13-mix.png`

A Bézier surface painted with a mix of cut copper in four stages of weathering, laid out in patches. Shuffle gives a new arrangement, and the hologram and the placed blocks keep the same one.
