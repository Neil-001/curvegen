Curve Generator turns 2D and 3D shapes into Minecraft builds. It picks slabs, stairs, walls, fences, panes and other pieces for you, so shapes are smoother and more accurate than those generated with full blocks alone, such as with other tools or mods.

Hold **V** to open the radial menu, or press **G** to open the full menu.

## Features

- **2D shapes**
  - Build ellipses, equations and Bézier curves.
- **3D shapes**
  - Build ellipsoids, toruses, 3D equations, Bézier curves and surfaces.
- **Editable hologram**
  - Resize, move and bend your shape in the world before placing it.
- **Connections**
  - Fences, panes, walls and stair corners follow Minecraft's connection rules.
- **Colour matching**
  - Pick a colour and let the mod choose blocks with the closest textures.
- **Block counts**
  - See how many of each block you'll need.
- **Presets**
  - Save shapes with their block choices.
- **Litematica export**
  - Save your shape as a `.litematic` schematic.
- **Server support**
  - Place builds and undo your last placement even on servers without the mod. You'll need operator rights.
